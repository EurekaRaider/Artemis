A closer look

From the first thought to the finished work.

An ongoing conversation, with the tools to turn it into something useful.

01

Projects & conversations

Keep the thread of your work.

Save, search, archive and fork conversations. Project tasks stay with your files; temporary chats give independent work its own space. Goals keep longer tasks focused, with optional token budgets.

02

Files & documents

Make something you can use.

Read and edit files, preview Markdown, and create Word, Excel, PowerPoint or PDF content with bundled plugins. These tools preserve basic content, not every macro, animation or complex layout.

03

Code & review

Understand the change before it leaves.

Compare the last turn, staged changes or a branch. Add inline comments, stage individual hunks, and work in your local checkout or an explicitly chosen isolated worktree.

04

Terminal & browser

Keep the evidence nearby.

Run a real terminal, open web pages, and inspect files beside your conversation. The browser keeps web content separate from desktop APIs and local files.

05

Agent teams

Give each part its own attention.

Let parallel agents investigate independent parts of a task. Create reusable specialists with their own instructions, model preferences and allowed tools; call them with @ and follow their progress.

06

Messaging & collaboration

Continue where the conversation happens.

Send tasks through WeCom, Feishu / Lark or Slack private chats. In Slack and Feishu / Lark groups, independently connected bots can share authorized work and return results to the requester. Your computer and Artemis must remain running.

07

Automations

Make space for a regular rhythm.

Schedule one-time, interval, daily or weekly work with a timezone and saved run history. Automations run while Artemis is open; Execute schedules require explicit authorization.

08

Plugins & connected services

Add what the work calls for.

Manage plugins, Skills, connectors and MCP in one place. Discover MCP servers, connect accounts or add a marketplace you trust. External marketplaces are not fetched by default.

09

Models & usage

Find your own balance.

Choose models per conversation, connect compatible providers and inspect token and cache usage. Ultra Mode brings deeper reasoning and parallel work to complex tasks, with higher usage.

10

Memory & language

Less repetition. More continuity.

Keep reusable project knowledge and instructions. Selectively import Skills and tool configuration without silently copying credentials. Work in any of 14 languages, with system-language support and an Arabic right-to-left layout.

Plugins & connected services

Your tools, together.

Discover, install and manage capabilities in the resource center. Connections stay optional, and their permissions stay visible.

Confidence starts with clarity.

Understand what stays local, what connects to a service, and what your chosen mode allows.
