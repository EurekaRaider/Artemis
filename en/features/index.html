IM group collaboration

One conversation. Everyone’s strengths.

In an existing Slack channel or Feishu / Lark group, each member connects their own bot and Artemis. Send work to the right person and the desktop capabilities they have chosen to share.

Give work to a specific member

Mention their bot in the group, or assign a task to a group member from Artemis.

Keep each person’s scope clear

Each computer grants access to its own projects, folders, files and operations.

Bring results back to the request

Delegated results return to the source group and mention the requester, ready for the next step.

Automatic delegation requires bot authorization and a verified round trip. People can also mention the next bot manually.

Your way of working. Roles you can call on.

Better when brought together

One set of methods. Two ways to collaborate.

Custom agents bring expertise. Agent Team coordinates work on one computer. IM groups connect different members’ Artemis workspaces.

Turn your specialist roles into a team.

Split research and review into independent tasks for custom agents to work on in parallel. The main agent coordinates progress and brings the results together. Follow each role in the team panel.

One request in the group. A team behind it.

Enable automatic invocation for local roles. When a group task reaches the member’s Artemis, its main agent can select those roles within the project’s scope. Each computer handles its part and returns results to the shared conversation.

  1. 01

    Ask in the group

    Give a goal to an authorized bot.

  2. 02

    Assign to a member

    Send the right part to that member’s Artemis.

  3. 03

    Coordinate locally

    The main agent calls available specialists and organizes its local team.

  4. 04

    Reply in the group

    Combine the results, reply to the requester and continue together.
